# Summary

Eyes on Karhutla Web is the public, read-only map product for the Eyes on Karhutla project (harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS hot spots). It shows satellite-detected burning activity across Indonesia as **active cell-days**, with MODIS and VIIRS S-NPP side by side and never summed. It is the `web/` package of the `eyes-on-karhutla` monorepo. All science lives in the Python package `../pipeline`, which exports static multi-year data products (contract v1) into `public/data/v1/`. This package only loads, validates and displays them. Current state: a full-screen national map (MapLibre on OpenFreeMap, locked to a viewport-sized box around Indonesia with everything else masked, labels drawn above the colours) shows the 34 province polygons, shaded by one sensor at a time (MODIS or VIIRS S-NPP toggle, each with its own scale and colour ramp; never combined). Hovering shows a card with MODIS and VIIRS S-NPP active cell-days side by side. Clicking or the picker selects a province, opens the drawer on it and flies the camera to it. A mini legend always stays on the map, and the credits are one tiny line on the bottom edge (full wording in the drawer). A ☰ menu (right of the ID/EN switch) opens a non-modal drawer combining the sensor toggle + legend, the province picker with national or province totals and the metric, coverage and boundary notes. Data comes through `src/data/`: typed loaders and React hooks, with types and validators generated from the exported JSON Schemas, and a visible error state instead of the map when loading fails. UI text is in Bahasa Indonesia (default) and English, with an ID/EN switch pinned top right. Selecting a province also loads its cells as dots coloured by sensor agreement (MODIS only / VIIRS S-NPP only / both; partial-only as rings; other-VIIRS-only hidden behind an opt-in switch), and clicking a dot shows its dates × sensors in the drawer. A year picker (default: the latest complete year, or "Semua tahun") drives everything: province colours on one scale per sensor fixed across years, figures (Indonesia = sum of provinces, outside provinces shown apart), and per-year dots. The drawer also charts monthly MODIS and VIIRS S-NPP active cell-days over all years (uPlot, one shared axis, gaps for missing months, the picked year shaded, click a month to pick its year). Every view is a shareable link (`?province=…&year=…&sensor=…&cell=…&other=1`), the address bar follows the view, and the drawer offers Copy link and a Copy citation that records the export date and pipeline commit ([ui/share-links.md](ui/share-links.md)). The product is **web (desktop) first**, but must stay usable at narrow widths.
